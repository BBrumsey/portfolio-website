My Projects

  • Ghost Heist

    Ghost Heist is a 2D platformer I developed in Unity, inspired by collecting treasures in Sly 2: Band of Thieves. I set up guards to patrol back and forth through the levels. Players have to time their jumps to avoid the guards and reach the treasure at the end.
