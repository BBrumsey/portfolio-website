About Me

Hey there! I’m Byron Jones, a game designer and developer studying Digital Game Development at The City College of New York. I earned my associate degree in Computer Science from Borough of Manhattan Community College before transferring to CCNY.

I enjoy level design, gameplay programming, and UI design. Working in Unity and C#, I’ve developed game prototypes and contributed to team projects. I also create 3D assets in Blender and work on art in Aseprite.

One of my current projects is Circuit Runner. I especially enjoyed designing the circuit players follow through the level, and I’m now working on its menus and interface. Seeing those parts come together has helped me discover how much I enjoy UI design.

I’m drawn to games that involve cooperation, teamwork, puzzles, and logical thinking. I enjoy thinking about how a level encourages players to observe a challenge, figure out an approach, and put their plan into action.

I enjoy playing games like Monster Hunter, Sonic the Hedgehog, and Sly Cooper.

Outside of development, I enjoy playing video games, listening to music, and doing voiceover work.
