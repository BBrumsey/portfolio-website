About Me

Hi, I’m Byron Jones, a game designer and developer studying Digital Game Development at The City College of New York. Before transferring to CCNY, I earned an associate degree in Computer Science from Borough of Manhattan Community College.

I’m currently working on Circuit Runner, a game where I designed the route players follow through the level. Laying out the circuit was one of my favorite parts, and I’ve enjoyed seeing the game come together as I build its menus and interface. I’m especially drawn to co-op games where players have to work together and puzzle games where they have to think through a challenge.
